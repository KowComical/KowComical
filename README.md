## About me

I'm a researcher and data engineer based in Melbourne, Australia, working across environmental data systems and artificial intelligence. I build automated data pipelines, research datasets, and interfaces that make Earth-system data easier to explore.

My work includes carbon emissions and power-sector monitoring, geospatial and time-series modelling, and explainable AI. I previously worked on the Carbon Monitor Project at Tsinghua University and completed a Master of Artificial Intelligence at Monash University.

**Tools I work with:** Python · SQL · FastAPI · Streamlit · Xarray · NetCDF · Linux
